感知之間 Between Perception

感知，是否比真相更重要？AI 正逐漸改變創作、學習與陪伴的方式，也重新定義人類與智慧的關係。
本展區透過三個系列作品，從不同面向展開這場對話。
《碳矽之間》探討創作與生成的界線；
《球球變勇敢》探索 AI 是否能理解勇氣、情感與生命經驗；
《同行者》思考當 AI 不再只是工具，而成為共同生活的存在，人類將迎來怎樣的未來。
三個系列沒有提供答案，而是邀請觀眾以自己的觀看經驗，重新定義人與 AI 的關係。

Is perception more important than truth?

As artificial intelligence increasingly participates in creation, learning, and companionship, it is also reshaping the relationship between humanity and intelligence itself.

This section presents three series, each approaching the future of humans and AI from a different perspective.

Between Carbon and Silicon examines the evolving boundary between human creativity and machine generation.
The Ball Learns Courage imagines whether artificial intelligence can understand courage, emotion, and lived experience.
Companions explores a future in which AI is no longer merely a tool, but a partner in everyday life.

Rather than offering definitive answers, these three series invite each visitor to reflect on how humanity may redefine its relationship with AI in the age of intelligent machines.
