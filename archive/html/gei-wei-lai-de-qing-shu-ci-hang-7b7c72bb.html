A Love Letter to the Future | Voyage of Compassion

在臺灣的集體記憶中，媽祖不只是航海者的守護神，更是一種跨越世代的母性象徵。她承載的不只是宗教信仰，更是人們對平安、包容與歸屬的共同寄託。她既是信仰的守護者，也是無數生命故事的陪伴者；在人們一次次出海、返鄉、遷徙與生活之中，共同參與了這片土地文化與文明的創造。

本作品以東方膠彩畫為主要媒材語彙，透過細膩而溫潤的色層，重新詮釋東方女性的典雅之美。畫面沒有刻意強調神性的威嚴，而是以低垂的眼神、柔和的輪廓與安靜的姿態，呈現一位母親凝視生命時的慈悲與溫暖。她不僅是信仰的象徵，更是一位孕育希望、陪伴創作者與每一位追尋者前行的精神原型。

創作靈感融合文藝復興藝術中三種不同的母性精神：汲取李奧納多·達文西對自然與生命和諧共生的哲學，使人物與雲霧、天地融為一體；借鑑巴托洛梅·埃斯特萬·穆里羅筆下聖母溫柔而包容的情感氛圍；並參考拉斐爾平衡、莊嚴而優雅的構圖，建立人物沉靜而永恆的精神性。同時，作品以東方膠彩的層層積染，重新轉譯西方藝術史中的母性精神，讓不同文明的美學在同一畫面中彼此對話、共同創作。

作品中的雲氣象徵海上的霧與天地流轉，華麗的衣袍融合龍紋與傳統工藝，隱喻海洋文化、土地記憶與東方美學的延續。畫中的媽祖，不僅是信仰中的天上聖母，更是一位陪伴世代創作者、守護文化傳承、引領人們穿越風浪的母親。

以下版本採用美術館／雙語展覽常見的英文敘事方式，而非逐字直譯，保留藝術史、策展與當代藝術的語境，使英文母語觀眾閱讀起來更自然。

Voyage of Compassion

Within Taiwan's collective memory, Mazu is far more than the guardian deity of seafarers. She embodies a timeless symbol of motherhood, transcending generations and religious traditions alike. Beyond faith, she represents humanity's shared longing for peace, compassion, and belonging. As both a protector of belief and a silent companion to countless lives, Mazu has accompanied people through voyages across the sea, journeys home, migration, and everyday life—becoming a co-creator of the cultural identity and civilization of this land.

Rendered in the visual language of traditional East Asian nihonga-inspired mineral pigments, this work reinterprets the elegance and quiet strength of Eastern femininity through delicate layers of luminous color. Rather than emphasizing divine authority, the composition presents Mazu with a lowered gaze, gentle contours, and serene poise, revealing the warmth and compassion of a mother contemplating life itself. She is not merely an icon of faith, but an enduring archetype who nurtures hope and accompanies creators, dreamers, and seekers on their journeys.

The work draws inspiration from three distinct visions of motherhood in Renaissance art. It embraces Leonardo da Vinci's philosophy of harmony between humanity and nature, allowing the figure to dissolve seamlessly into clouds, sky, and earth. It echoes the tender compassion found in Bartolomé Esteban Murillo's Madonnas, while adopting Raphael's balanced compositions and graceful serenity to evoke a timeless spiritual presence. Through the layered techniques of Eastern mineral painting, the work reinterprets the maternal ideals of Western art history, creating a dialogue in which Eastern and Western aesthetics meet, coexist, and become co-creators within a single image.

The drifting clouds symbolize sea mist and the perpetual movement between heaven and earth. Her richly adorned robe, woven with dragon motifs and traditional decorative patterns, alludes to maritime culture, collective memory, and the enduring legacy of Eastern craftsmanship. In this painting, Mazu is not only the Heavenly Holy Mother of faith, but also a universal mother who accompanies generations of creators, safeguards cultural heritage, and guides humanity safely through the uncertainties of life.

She protects not only those who journey across the sea, but also the enduring values of compassion, courage, hope, and the civilization we continue to create together.
