共創之間 Between Co-Creation

人工智慧並非取代創作者，而是成為新的創作媒介。

本展區透過藝術共創計畫《給未來的情書》，以及《文明的典藏：往昔異鄉》系列，邀請來自產業、政府、學術、文化與藝術領域的參與者，共同完成跨越世代與媒介的創作。

Artificial intelligence is not presented here as a replacement for human creativity, but as a new creative medium.

Through A Love Letter to the Future and Past Is a Foreign Land, this gallery brings together voices from industry, academia, government, and the arts to explore new forms of collaborative creation.
