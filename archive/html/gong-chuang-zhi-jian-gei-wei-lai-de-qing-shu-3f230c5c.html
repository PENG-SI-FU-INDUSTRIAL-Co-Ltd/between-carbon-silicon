共創之間 | 給未來的情書

人工智慧正重新定義創作，也重新定義我們所處的時代。

《給未來的情書》邀請來自產業、政府與學術界的代表，以一句話寫下對未來世界的想像、信念、提醒或期許。這些文字不只是個人的理念，更是屬於 2026 年的時代印記。

策展團隊以每位參與者的文字為核心，結合生成式人工智慧進行藝術轉譯，將抽象的思想轉化為獨一無二的當代藝術作品。每一件作品，都是人類思想與人工智慧共同創作的成果，也是這個世代留給未來的一封情書。

當多年以後再次閱讀這些作品，它們將不只是藝術，更是一座記錄文明、價值與時代精神的數位時光膠囊。

A Love Letter to the Future

Artificial intelligence is redefining not only creativity, but also the era we live in.

A Love Letter to the Future brings together leaders from industry, government, and academia, inviting each participant to leave a single sentence—a hope, a belief, a question, or a message for future generations. These words become lasting reflections of the values and aspirations of our time.

Using each participant's statement as the creative foundation, the curatorial team collaborates with generative AI to transform ideas into original contemporary artworks. Every piece represents a dialogue between human imagination and artificial intelligence, preserving a unique perspective on the present.

As time passes, these works become more than art. They become a digital time capsule—preserving the thoughts, values, and cultural memory of 2026 for those who will one day look back upon our era.
